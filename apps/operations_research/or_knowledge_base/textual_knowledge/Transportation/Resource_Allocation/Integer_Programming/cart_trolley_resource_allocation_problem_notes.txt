Problem Description:
A concert organizer must transport equipment using carts and trolleys with different capacities and worker requirements. Objective is to minimize the total number of workers, with constraints including minimum number of trolleys, rate requirements, and share-of-method constraints.

Modeling Notes:
- Integer decision variables for numbers of carts and trolleys.
- Objective: Minimize total workers required.
- Constraints:
    * Minimum trolleys used.
    * Transport rate must meet/exceed demand.
    * No more than X% of transport provided by method Y (share constraint).
    * All variables integer/bounded.
- Suitable for algebraic modeling (Pyomo, mixed-integer programming).
- Example parameter values: carts (5kg/min, 2 workers), trolleys (7kg/min, 4 workers), required rate, method-share cap.

This knowledge is relevant for future problems involving resource allocation, capacity and share constraints, and can be used in machine assignment contexts.
