Title: Science Show Production Mix LP (Minty Foam/Black Tar)

Problem statement:
A science show performs two demonstrations: each uses mint and active ingredient to produce minty foam and black tar. Demo 1 uses 10 mint, 20 active, yields 25 foam, and 5 black tar; demo 2 uses 12 mint, 15 active, yields 18 foam, and 3 black tar. Limits: 120 mint, 100 active, 50 black tar. Objective: maximize total minty foam produced by choosing non-negative real numbers of each demonstration.

LP Formulation:
Let x1: number of demo 1 performed
Let x2: number of demo 2 performed

Maximize: 25*x1 + 18*x2
subject to:
10*x1 + 12*x2 <= 120     (mint)
20*x1 + 15*x2 <= 100     (active ingredient)
5*x1 + 3*x2 <= 50        (black tar)
x1 >= 0, x2 >= 0

Optimal solution (continuous): max foam = 125.0 units, achieved by x1 = 5.0, x2 = 0.0
See code: minty_foam_lp.py (Pyomo, GLPK)

Metadata:
- LP, production mix, resource constraints, Pyomo/GLPK code example.
