# Reference: Baby Food Blending Linear Program Example

## Description
This file provides a reference example of a linear programming (LP) model for blending or proportional-ratio-constrained dietary problems, specifically formulated for baby food mix design. Such models are prevalent in operations research when addressing problems of food formulation, blending, or feeding regime optimization, especially where proportions (relative quantities of ingredients) or upper/lower bounds on intake must be maintained.

## Use cases
- Feeding or dietary mix maximization under proportionality or blending constraints
- Resource allocation in the food industry, agriculture, livestock feed, or any context with ingredient ratios
- Problems aiming to meet minimum/maximum intake requirements for nutrients or components
- Educational and illustrative example for LP with bounded intake and proportional mix
